Two cafés. Same welcome.

The kind of café you end up in twice a day.

Happy Sheep is an independent coffee shop with two spots in Dublin, one in Dundrum Business Park and one on Terenure Road North. We make good coffee, we bake, we do a bit of lunch, and we sell the kind of mugs you buy for a friend and then keep for yourself.
